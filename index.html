Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية

Last updated: 12 July 2026

The short version: Athan Cast does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no accounts. Everything stays on your device.

Who we are

Athan Cast is an independent, ad-free prayer-times application built by a solo developer. This policy explains — plainly — what the app does and does not do with information.

Data we collect

None. Athan Cast has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your preferences locally on your phone so it can work. This never leaves your device:

  • Your chosen city or coordinates, used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction
  • Your settings: calculation method, school of Fiqh, adjustments, language, and theme
  • Your selected Athan sounds and notification preferences
  • The name of a speaker you chose for casting
  • Widget appearance settings

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location — used only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. Your location is never sent anywhere. You may instead choose a city manually.
  • Internet / Network / Wi-Fi — used to discover and connect to speakers on your local network for casting the Athan. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Notifications, alarms, and background running — used to deliver the Athan and prayer reminders at the correct times.

Casting

When you cast the Athan, audio is sent from your device to the speaker you selected on your own local network. Nothing passes through us, and nothing is recorded.

Purchases

Athan Cast offers a single, optional one-time contribution. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback, the app opens your own email application with a message you can review and edit before sending. Basic technical details (app version, device model, Android version, and recent casting logs) are included to help diagnose problems. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.

Third parties

Athan Cast contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries. City data is provided by GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and is bundled with the app — no requests are made to their servers. Google Play services are used only for casting and for processing an optional purchase.

Children

Athan Cast is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions are welcome: athancastapp@gmail.com
